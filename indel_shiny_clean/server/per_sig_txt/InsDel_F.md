InsDel_F and ID_F are primarily characterized by 2bp deletions arising from short tandem repeats in which the deleted sequence is repeated 1 to 3 times, or from deletions associated with 1bp microhomology. Previous studies have shown that this mutational process depends on TOP1‑mediated cleavage at genome‑embedded ribonucleotides under conditions of RNase H2 deficiency, and has been validated in yeast, human cell lines, and mouse models (Reijns et al., Nature, 2022). In contrast to ID4/C_ID4, which exhibits a broader spectrum of 2–5bp deletions at short tandem repeats, ID_F and InsDel_F are specifically dominated by 2bp deletions and show a strong preference for mutations occurring at CTCT motifs, whereas C_ID4 is preferentially associated with TTTT motifs.
